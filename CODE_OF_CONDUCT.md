# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation welcoming and respectful regardless of experience,
background, identity, nationality, disability, religion, or personal circumstances.
This applies to project discussions, reviews, issues, and other spaces maintained
by the project, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and provide constructive, specific feedback.
- Discuss technical disagreements using evidence and focus on the work.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to end an interaction.
- Credit others' contributions and make room for different perspectives.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct their impact where possible.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, intimidation,
personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent
are unacceptable. Do not post credentials or sensitive infrastructure data.

## Reporting

Contact a project maintainer privately through a contact method they have
published. A dedicated conduct-reporting address has not yet been established.
Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues. Include the context,
relevant messages or links, and any immediate concerns, sharing only information
needed to assess the incident. For software vulnerabilities, use the separate
[security reporting process](security.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share information only as needed
to investigate, respond, or meet applicable obligations. Absolute confidentiality
and response timelines cannot be guaranteed. A maintainer involved in an incident
should not be its sole reviewer; where possible, contact another maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and request that conduct stop.
Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a private warning,
a request for corrective action, temporary participation restrictions, or removal
from project spaces. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate removal.
Maintainers should explain decisions privately when appropriate and consider
relevant context. Anyone seeking reconsideration can contact an uninvolved
maintainer, where available.
